Privacy Policy for Aero Music

Aero Music ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We use Google Sign-In to authenticate users. We only access your basic profile information (name, email, profile picture) to identify you within the app. We do not access your Google Drive, Contacts, YouTube account data, or any other private data.

How We Use Information

Your information is used solely to provide app functionality such as login, saving your favorites, playlists, and listening history locally on your device.

Data Storage

Your favorites, playlists, and history are stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Aero Music uses publicly available YouTube content for music playback and public APIs (such as Wikipedia and Spotify's public web data) for artist information and lyrics. These services have their own privacy policies.
